What connects to the internet

Model catalogs and downloads, Agent searches and webpage reading request third-party services. Ordinary answers are still generated locally. Providers can generally receive your IP address, request time, URL and headers, and may retain records.

Model catalogs and downloads

The catalog comes from meta.alicdn.com; opening or refreshing it can make a request. Model files come from your selected ModelScope, Hugging Face or Modelers source. Some metadata may use HF Mirror.

Requests contain model IDs and file paths, generally without chat text, and may redirect to storage or delivery hosts. HF Mirror is a separate service; its operator and rules should not be assumed to be those of Hugging Face.

ModelScope · Hugging Face Privacy Policy · Modelers · HF Mirror

Agent search

Search currently tries Sogou (m.sogou.com), 360 Search (m.so.com) and Bing (www.bing.com) in order. If results are insufficient, the same query is sent to later services.

Queries may contain your entire question or information from context or long-term memory. Providers also receive connection details. The local model processes the returned results.

Sogou Privacy Policy · 360 Search Privacy Policy · Microsoft Privacy Statement, including Bing

Webpages, email and sharing

Agent visits URLs you provide or select from search results, and may follow redirects. Websites receive the URL’s path and query parameters, including any personal information or credentials embedded in them.

Feedback and sharing use your selected mail or other app. Feedback includes only your description, app version and iOS version by default; any attachments you add also reach the recipient.

Third-party handling

Check each provider’s current information for retention, processing locations and rights-request channels. Some platforms or websites may use servers outside mainland China. This page does not obtain blanket consent for third-party processing or remove our legal duties. Email tangesion@163.com with questions.
