Privacy at a glance

Ordinary conversations in MNN Chat run on your device, and chats are saved locally. Model downloads, Agent searches and webpage reading use the internet.

The current version is free, with no account required, advertising tracking, automatic usage analytics or crash uploads. System diagnostics, backups and sharing follow their own settings.

Local conversations

Your text, model responses and supported images, audio, video or files are used on your device to generate, display and save conversations. Some media creates temporary files. A local model may summarize older messages and save the summary with the chat.

Local chat does not send inputs to a cloud model to generate answers, and we do not obtain chats through this feature for training. Model, language and inference settings stay on the device. Storage availability, errors and performance information help manage downloads and operation.

Model catalogs and downloads

Opening or refreshing the model catalog requests an online list, even before you download anything. Downloads send the selected platform a model ID and file path; they generally do not need chat text.

Catalog, download and delivery services can receive your IP address, request time, headers and other connection details. See Online Services for the current providers and links.

Agent searches and webpages

Agent sends queries to search providers and reads selected webpages. A query may contain your entire question, or information from the conversation or long-term memory. If the model supplies no valid query, the app may use your question. If results are insufficient, the same query may reach several providers.

Search providers receive queries and connection details. Websites receive URL paths, query parameters and connection details. Avoid unnecessary identity documents, health or financial information, other people’s private information, and URLs containing credentials.

These services may keep request records. Some platforms or websites use servers outside mainland China; locations and retention depend on the provider. Reading this policy or enabling Agent is not blanket consent to all processing. Separate notice, consent or other arrangements must still be completed where required.

Agent memory

Agent can save preferences, facts and reusable procedures locally for later conversations. Saves may be initiated by the model or by your explicit request to remember something. Saved information may also help generate search queries.

Chats and memory are stored separately. Deleting a chat does not clear memory, and clearing memory does not delete the original chat. View and clear it in Settings → Agent Memory and Skills. Current conversations may still contain loaded information, so stop generation and start a new conversation after clearing.

Agent mode belongs to the conversation. To stop using it, stop generation and start a new ordinary chat without enabling Agent. This does not stop catalog or download requests.

Permissions and sharing

The camera captures input, Photos selects or saves media, and the microphone records voice input. Manage permissions in iOS Settings; text chat remains available without these features. File access is limited to files you select.

Copying a message writes it to the system clipboard. Saving or sharing content passes it to Photos, Files, Mail or another app you choose. Check the content and recipient first.

Storage and deletion

Chats, attachments, summaries, models, settings and memory are kept in the app’s local data area. Chats and memory normally remain until deleted, cleared, updated or replaced; closing the app does not erase them. Downloads and model operation can create temporary files and caches.

Delete chats in History and downloaded models in the model list. Bundled models may not be individually removable. Removing a model does not delete chats or memory.

Local deletion does not remove clipboard contents, shared copies, content in other apps, backups or third-party records, and does not guarantee irreversible physical erasure. The app has no chat cloud sync of its own. iCloud and computer backups follow system settings; restoring an old backup can restore earlier data. Offloading an app keeps its documents and differs from deleting it.

Local data uses iOS app isolation and permissions. Protect device access, backups and sharing. These mechanisms do not mean every copy is encrypted or remove every security risk.

Feedback and privacy requests

The feedback form contains your description, app version and iOS version, without automatically attaching chats. Email also sends your address and any attachments you add so we can investigate and reply. We keep these details until the issue and necessary follow-up are complete, then delete or anonymize them, except for information needed by law or for a dispute.

To ask about or request access, a copy, correction, supplementation, deletion, restriction or withdrawal of consent for personal information we actually handle, contact tangesion@163.com. We will verify necessary details and respond promptly under applicable law. If we cannot fulfill a request, we will explain why and what options are available. Do not send passwords, verification codes or unrelated chats.

We cannot remotely view or delete data stored only on your device, but can help you manage it. Withdrawing consent stops subsequent processing based on that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Use providers’ policy channels for third-party records; we will assist where required. There is no app account to close.

Minors

The app is not specifically designed for children. Minors should use it with guardian guidance. Handling personal information about children under fourteen requires applicable guardian consent and protection; this policy alone does not obtain that consent.

If you or a guardian believe information has been handled improperly, email us so we can investigate and take necessary action.

Policy changes

We update this policy when features or data handling change. Material changes to purposes, data types or recipients require notice in advance and renewed consent where required. Updates do not retroactively authorize past processing.
